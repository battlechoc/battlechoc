## Policies

- **Draft v0.2:** Halving every 2 years, a fixed fee of 20 CHOC once 50 % is issued, the whole pot paid out.
- **Dynamic fee:** The fee is the highest multiple of 20 (0 to 100 CHOC) that 85 % of interested players could pay last season.
- **Free entry:** Tournaments never charge a registration fee.
- **Pot reserve:** Each season pays out only 75 % of the pot.
- **Smooth 4-year halving:** Issuance shrinks every season and halves every 4 years instead of halving in steps every 2 years.
- **Pantry:** Prizes stay in a player-owned contract until withdrawn; what departed players left there returns to the pot after 2 years.
- **Free qualifiers:** Like the recommendation, but the best 10 % of the open ladder enter the paid season tournament for free.
- **Recommended:** Smooth 4-year halving, the pantry, and a fixed fee of 20 CHOC once 50 % of the supply is issued (whitepaper v1.0).
